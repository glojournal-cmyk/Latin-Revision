How to update GitHub Pages
--------------------------
Upload these files over the existing files in your repository:
- index.html
- app.js
- sw.js
- manifest.webmanifest

question-bank.js is included only for completeness and is unchanged.

Keep your existing icons/ folder.

Before updating
---------------
Use Parent controls -> Backup progress first.

After updating
--------------
1. Refresh the GitHub Pages URL in Safari.
2. Fully close the installed Latin Revision app.
3. Reopen it from the iPad Home Screen.
4. The header should show Reliable Practice V8.1.

Daily goal behaviour
--------------------
The app marks the day complete only when BOTH are met:
- at least 20 active study minutes
- at least 30 questions answered

Closing or hiding the app pauses the active timer. Extended inactivity also pauses it.
The goal does not stop extra practice after completion.
